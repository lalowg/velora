    Reel 0400:02:58:20

    From croquisto coat.

    1. 01

      Croquis

      A single figure, drawn ten heads tall. We look for the line the garment will make in shadow, not the one it makes in light.

    2. 02

      Pattern

      The line becomes flat pieces — front, back, sleeve, collar. Grain lines are set so the cloth falls, rather than hangs.

    3. 03

      Toile & cut

      Cut first in calico, fitted on the stand, corrected in pencil. Only then does the melton meet the shears.

    4. 04

      Drape

      Assembled, pressed, weighted at the hem. The coat is finished when it moves a moment after you do.

    The archive

    Seven collections, each one a short film and every look a single frame. A fictional archive for a fictional house.

    1. Half-light tailoring and liquid silk, shown as a four-scene film. The current collection.

    2. Bleached linens and sheer layers, left to dry stiff in sea air before they were cut.

    3. Tulle veils over severe wool. Ceremony, with the ceremony taken out.

    4. Smoke-dyed cotton and wide brims for long, pale afternoons.

    5. Hand-lacquered capes and black-on-black embroidery that only shows when you move.

    6. One colour, one cloth, cut every way it would allow.

    7. The debut: nine hooded columns, shown in a room with the lights switched off.
